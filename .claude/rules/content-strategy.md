# Content Strategy

Voice and content rules for portfolio copy. Every word should be intentional.

---

## Voice

- **Direct.** No fluff, no buzzwords ("synergy", "leverage", "cutting-edge").
- **Confident, not arrogant.** State capability plainly. Let projects speak.
- **Editorial.** Treat copy like print typography — line breaks matter.
- **Personal.** This is a portfolio, not a company site. First-person is allowed.
- **Slightly playful, never cute.** Pixel Melbourne energy filtered through Adjaycent restraint.

---

## Hero copy

Three lines, no more:

1. **Eyebrow** (mono, micro): a positioning tag, e.g. `Builder · Engineer · Designer`
2. **Display headline**: 4–10 words. A statement, not a description.
3. **Positioning line**: 12–24 words. What I do, who it serves, why it's different.

Plus: one primary CTA ("See selected work") and one secondary ("Get in touch").

---

## Project card content (Selected Work)

Every project entry must include:

- `title` — short, recognizable
- `type` — one of: `Product`, `System`, `Tool`, `Experiment`, `Brand`
- `role` — verb-led (e.g. `Lead engineer`, `Designed and built`, `Architected`)
- `year` — 4-digit
- `tags` — 2–4 capabilities used
- `impact` — ONE sentence. What changed because of this work.
- `thumbnail` — high-quality, consistent aspect (16:10), neutral or branded
- `link` (optional) — case study URL or live site
- `featured` — boolean. Featured items appear larger in the grid.

Card UI shows: thumbnail, title, type, role, impact. Tags appear on hover.

---

## Capabilities

Editorial list, not a feature grid. Group into 3 buckets:

- **Engineering** — languages, frameworks, systems
- **Design & UX** — interfaces, motion, type, prototyping
- **Direction** — strategy, leadership, scoping, mentoring

Each bullet: one short phrase, no period. Avoid version numbers.

---

## About

Two short paragraphs. Maximum.

- Para 1 — what I do and how I work.
- Para 2 — what drives me / what I'm thinking about now.

Plus a single quote or aphorism (Adjaycent-style symbolic break) between them.

---

## Contact

- Email (large, copyable, mono)
- 2–3 social links (icons only, labeled for screen readers)
- One closing line ("Currently open to: …" or "Based in …")

No form. No CAPTCHA. No "let's connect" buttons. Mailto.

---

## Tone test

A line passes if I can imagine reading it aloud confidently. A line fails if it reads like a bullet from a job description.
