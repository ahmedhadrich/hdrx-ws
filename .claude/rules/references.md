# References — Audit Summary

These references are the design DNA. We extract patterns, never copy. Each entry below distills the audit conducted in Phase 1.

---

## Fluid Glass — https://fluid.glass/  (40% — primary)

**Take**: Structure, restraint, layered premium feel.

- Modular card system with alternating full-width imagery and contained content.
- Neutral-dominant palette (whites, grays, charcoals); high-contrast photography injects color.
- Heavy reliance on glass surfaces, soft shadows, generous whitespace.
- Smooth progressive reveals on scroll; staggered entrance animations.
- Photography-centric backgrounds — natural materials carry the texture.

**Apply**: Base palette, glass surfaces on cards, generous spacing, alternating section layouts in Selected Work.

---

## Mad Creative Agency — https://mad-creative-agency.com/  (20%)

**Take**: Hero impact, declarative typography, visual confidence.

- Hero is text-forward: bold tagline → supporting copy → CTA.
- Large, confident sans-serif headlines (48px+).
- Light/dark duality. No garish accents in hero.
- Punchy, declarative voice ("We think big. Then build loud.").
- "DRAG" carousel suggests horizontal exploration of work.

**Apply**: Hero composition formula — headline + clarifying line + CTA + atmosphere. Confident type sizes. No clutter.

---

## Pixel Melbourne — https://pixel.melbourne/  (15%)

**Take**: Gallery treatment with character.

- Director cards: avatar + name + minimal CTA. Clean, repeatable.
- Conversational, energetic voice ("make ideas fizzzzz with life").
- Animated GIFs as visual breaks.
- 8-card grid with equal visual weight.
- Capability tags with icon/emoji pairing.

**Apply**: Selected Work card pattern (image/thumb + title + type + role + impact). Equal-weight grid. Personality in micro-copy.

---

## HUPR — https://hupr.ca/  (10%)

**Take**: Colorful section transitions, scroll rhythm.

- Hierarchical sections with strong horizontal rule dividers.
- Light backgrounds, dark type, photography drives accent color.
- Service cards consistent (image + title + body).
- Newsletter and CTA arrow patterns.

**Apply**: Section dividers and rhythm. Selective color breaks between otherwise-restrained sections. Arrow CTAs.

---

## Shader — https://www.shader.se/  (10%)

**Take**: Retro-tech signature.

- Bold positioning ("Plugged into the future") with conversational warmth.
- Audit yielded little visual data — the brand promise of "shader" is the cue, not the execution.
- Patterns we'd extract: subtle scanline overlays, glitch on hover, phosphor-glow CTAs, cassette-style loaders.

**Apply**: ONE signature retro-tech moment in the site. Choose: hero media, project hover, or transition into 3D. Never site-wide.

---

## Adjaycent — https://www.adjaycent.work/  (5%)

**Take**: Editorial typography attitude.

- Bold declarative headers with deliberate line breaks.
- Mixed-case phrases ("Intent In Form").
- Symbolic markers (hazard glyphs, geometric icons) as editorial punctuation.
- Asymmetric alignment, breathing space.
- Heavy weight contrast.

**Apply**: Capabilities and About voice. Symbolic glyph dividers (sparingly). Asymmetric type compositions for accents.

---

## Loud — https://loudsrl.com/  (secondary, ambient)

**Take**: Ambient supporting layer.

- Subtle parallax, soft gradient overlays.
- Staggered section reveals — never aggressive.
- Restrained micro-interactions.
- Industry-tile rhythm with image-first loading.

**Apply**: Site-wide ambient background — soft drifting noise/gradient layer. Section reveal staggering. Never the focus.

---

## Cross-Reference Synthesis

The portfolio is, in one sentence:
> Fluid Glass structure carrying Mad Creative confidence, Pixel Melbourne playfulness, HUPR rhythm, one Shader retro signature, Adjaycent editorial sharpness, atop a Loud ambient layer.

When in doubt: **restraint wins**. The 40% Fluid Glass mandate means quiet is the default and loud is the accent.
